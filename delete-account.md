# Delete Your MinnalHelp Account

MinnalHelp allows users to permanently delete their account and associated personal information.

## Delete Your Account From the App

You can delete your account directly from the MinnalHelp app:

1. Open **MinnalHelp**.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Go to the **Home** screen.
4. Select **Delete account**.
5. Review the account deletion confirmation.
6. Select **Delete account** again to confirm.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the MinnalHelp app, you can request deletion by contacting:

**[roopa.inbox@gmail.com](mailto:roopa.inbox@gmail.com)**

Please include the phone number associated with your MinnalHelp account.

We may need to verify that you are the account owner before processing the deletion request.

## What We Delete

When your MinnalHelp account is deleted, personal information associated with your account is deleted where applicable, including:

* Your MinnalHelp authentication account
* Your profile information
* Your name and phone number
* Community and address-related profile information
* Your profile photo, if provided
* Helper service associations
* Service requests associated with your account
* Job interests, assignments, reviews, and related account activity associated with your account

## What Is Not Deleted

Shared application data that does not belong specifically to your account is not deleted. This includes information such as:

* Communities
* Blocks
* Service categories
* Data belonging to other MinnalHelp users

We may retain limited information where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.
