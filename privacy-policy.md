# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

MinnalHelp ("MinnalHelp", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using the MinnalHelp mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and protected, and the choices available to you.

By using MinnalHelp, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. About MinnalHelp

MinnalHelp is a community-based platform designed to connect residents with local service helpers for services such as household assistance and other community services available through the App.

Residents can create service requests, and eligible helpers can view available requests and express their interest in providing the requested service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Phone Number and Authentication Information

MinnalHelp uses phone-number-based authentication.

We may collect and process:

* Mobile phone number
* Authentication identifiers
* Information necessary to verify and maintain your authenticated session

Your phone number is used primarily to authenticate your account and provide access to MinnalHelp.

### Profile Information

Depending on how you use MinnalHelp, we may collect profile information such as:

* Name
* User role or account type
* Community
* Block
* Floor number
* Flat number
* Services offered or selected
* Other information you voluntarily provide through your profile

The information collected may differ depending on whether you use MinnalHelp as a resident/customer or helper.

### Service Request Information

When you create or interact with a service request, we may process information such as:

* Requested service category
* Requested date and time
* Service-request status
* Community or location information associated with the request
* Helpers who express interest in a request
* Assignment information
* Information necessary to manage the service request

### Technical and Operational Information

We may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

This may include information generated by our authentication, hosting, database, security, or application infrastructure.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through MinnalHelp to:

* Create and authenticate user accounts
* Provide and operate MinnalHelp
* Maintain user profiles
* Connect residents with relevant helpers
* Display available service requests to eligible helpers
* Allow helpers to express interest in service requests
* Manage service-request assignment and status
* Maintain the security and integrity of the App
* Prevent misuse or unauthorized access
* Troubleshoot technical problems
* Provide user support
* Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use personal information for purposes materially unrelated to providing MinnalHelp without appropriate notice or consent where required.

## 4. Information Sharing

MinnalHelp does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared within the App when necessary to provide its core functionality. For example, information associated with a service request may be made available to relevant users so that residents and helpers can coordinate the requested service.

We may also share or process information through service providers that help us operate MinnalHelp.

These providers may provide services such as:

* Authentication
* Database infrastructure
* Cloud hosting
* SMS or OTP delivery
* Security and application infrastructure

We expect service providers to process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and subject to their applicable privacy and security obligations.

## 5. Supabase

MinnalHelp uses Supabase for backend services, including authentication and database functionality.

Information required to operate MinnalHelp may therefore be processed or stored using Supabase infrastructure.

Supabase's processing of information is also governed by its applicable privacy and security terms.

## 6. SMS and OTP Providers

MinnalHelp may use third-party communication providers to deliver one-time passwords (OTPs) used for phone-number verification.

Your phone number and information necessary to deliver the authentication message may be processed by these providers for this purpose.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled through MinnalHelp.

These measures may include authentication controls, database access controls, authorization policies, encrypted network communications, and restrictions on access to application data.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide MinnalHelp, maintain legitimate operational records, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the security and integrity of the service.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate, subject to legal, security, and operational requirements.

## 9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your MinnalHelp account and associated personal information by contacting:

**[support@minnalhelp.com](mailto:support@minnalhelp.com)**

Please send the request using the phone number or other account information necessary for us to identify and verify the account.

We may retain limited information where required for legal, fraud-prevention, security, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes permitted by applicable law.

## 10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the ability to request:

* Access to information associated with your account
* Correction of inaccurate information
* Deletion of your account and personal information
* Information about how your personal information is handled

To submit a privacy request, contact:

**[support@minnalhelp.com](mailto:support@minnalhelp.com)**

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

## 11. Children's Privacy

MinnalHelp is intended for adults who are seeking or providing community services.

MinnalHelp is not intended for children under the age required to independently consent to the processing of personal information under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for the purpose of creating MinnalHelp service accounts.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through MinnalHelp without appropriate authorization, please contact us at **[support@minnalhelp.com](mailto:support@minnalhelp.com)**.

## 12. Third-Party Services

MinnalHelp relies on third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate certain parts of the App.

Those providers may process limited information necessary to provide their services and may maintain their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

MinnalHelp is not responsible for independent services or websites that users access outside the MinnalHelp application.

## 13. International Processing

Some service providers used by MinnalHelp may process or store information using infrastructure located outside your state or country.
